This flow is the end-to-end L2 — the high-level steps consistent for this domain/channel. Use the Scenario selector to model variation: pick "Base (L2)" to build the shared skeleton, or "+ Scenario" to add a named scenario, then tag any step or connector as scenario-specific in "Configure" — those steps (including sub-process loops, via a self-connect) only appear for scenarios that need them, and each step's inputs/outputs/etc. can be overridden per scenario without touching the base. A pink dot marks a scenario-specific step; a gold dot means a specification is saved. In Current State, "Configure" lets you add any number of pain points per step, each attributed to a root cause — Process, System, Policy, or People — and linked to the reporting KPI(s) and value driver(s) it impacts; a red dot flags any step with pain documented. In Future State, "Configure" lets you check off which Current State step(s) a step relates to and set the business goal/outcome it's tracking toward — leave every box unchecked and the step gets a red GAP tag automatically, meaning that capability doesn't exist today. "Export spec (JSON)" includes a full pain/gap analysis summary for whichever view you're in. + Horizontal lane and + Vertical lane can both be used at once to form a real grid — the grid lines are a muted neutral tone, deliberately distinct from the teal/gold connector lines so they never get confused for process flow. Every connector's weight, dash pattern, and routing (straight, curved, or right-angle "orthogonal") is set per-connector in "Configure" — curved and orthogonal routing are the cleanest way to cross between lanes. When a step has two or more outgoing branches, they now share one trunk line out of the shape before forking to their targets. Save your work as a reusable template from the Template group — seed templates are built-in references, "My templates" are yours to edit, save, and delete.
