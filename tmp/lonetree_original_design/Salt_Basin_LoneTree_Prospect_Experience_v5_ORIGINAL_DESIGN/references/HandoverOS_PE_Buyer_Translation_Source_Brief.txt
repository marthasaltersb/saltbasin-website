HandoverOS / PE Buyer Translation — Source Brief
Prepared for the Salt Basin × LoneTree Capital prospect experience

Source context
This brief preserves the prior authored HandoverOS and PE Buyer Translation concepts referenced in the project conversation and the source named HandoverOS_PE_Buyer_Translation.xlsx.

Core translation
Operational Intelligence → Portfolio Intelligence → Fund Intelligence

HandoverOS purpose
Identify what is lost when work moves between teams and systems, especially from lead through pricing, quote, contract, order, entitlement, usage, invoice, revenue, collections, renewal and expansion.

Risk patterns
- Missing assumptions and metadata
- Contract interpretation gaps
- Pricing, entitlement and billing mismatches
- Reporting blind spots and reconciliation breaks
- Revenue leakage and cash-conversion friction
- Restatement, control and buyer-diligence risk

PE buyer translation
- Revenue leakage and recovery opportunity
- EBITDA, margin, cash flow and working-capital impact
- Repeatability of operating processes
- Sponsor reporting and value-creation attribution
- Exit readiness and reconstructability of claims

Typical outputs
- Handoff-risk map
- Revenue reconciliation findings
- Definition conflict inventory
- Leakage hypotheses and root-cause analysis
- Cash / AR friction analysis
- 100-day operating initiatives
- Measurement baselines and evidence requirements
- Role-aware CEO, CFO, GP and LP reporting

Commercial boundary
This source supports operating, evidence and communications design. It does not authorize broker-dealer activity, investor solicitation or transaction-contingent compensation.
