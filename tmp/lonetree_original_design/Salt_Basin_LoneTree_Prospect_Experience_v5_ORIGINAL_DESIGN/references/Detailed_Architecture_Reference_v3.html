Authoritative Methodology

Belief becomes defensible proof.

The pasted chat context governs this edition. It defines the evidence chain, Cost to Produce Truth™, Salter Momentum™, the claim-safe operating thesis, and the product-to-outcome method.

METHOD

Prioritize → Underwrite → Measure → Attribute → Prove

Each stage adds a governed definition, event, control, calculation, or evidence relationship—not merely a presentation layer.

MOMENTUM

Understanding → Rendering → Manifesting

Salter Momentum™ describes how governed understanding becomes a visible operating world and then a repeatable outcome.

TRUTH COST

Claim ← Attribution ← Explanation ← Result ← Source

Cost to Produce Truth™ measures the friction required to traverse a claim backward through the complete governed chain.

Claim-safe operating thesis

Investment → defined capability → operational change → source events → reconciliation → EBITDA / ARR effect → attribution → exit evidence.

Valuation guardrail

Higher valuation is a hypothesis to validate, not a promise. Stronger evidence can improve defensibility and buyer responsiveness; external market pricing remains outside the platform’s control.

Crystal World

Governed state becomes geometry.

The homepage crystal remains the canonical brand anchor. The world extends it into atoms, molecules, rods, branches and convergence.

Crystal Atom

One bounded definition, evidence rule, validation state, confidence, lineage and temporal history.

Molecule

Related atoms grouped into a capability such as Pricing, Contract Terms, Billing or Security.

Journey Rod

Persistent end-to-end Revenue, Customer, Member or Data Channel journey state.

Branch

A first-class change journey with its own agent, atoms, evidence, decisions and maturity.

Convergence

Revenue, customer, member and thesis/evidence truth reconcile into one governed state.

Geometry

Completeness, confidence, evidence strength, risk and active work alter shape, clarity and stability.
