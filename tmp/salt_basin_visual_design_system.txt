1. BRAND THESIS
Salt Basin Net Works should not look like a standard resume template. It should look like an operating intelligence
system: part executive annual report, part PE value creation memo, part AI-native product studio. The design should
make complex transformation work feel structured, measurable, and calm.
CORE VISUAL METAPHOR
Use the basin/tributary/tide language as a unifying system: work enters as scattered tributaries, is structured through
operating channels, and becomes measurable enterprise value. This creates a proprietary visual language without
becoming gimmicky.
BRAND ADJECTIVES
Strategic, not corporate-generic.
Analytical, not cold.
Modern, not trendy.
Premium, not decorative.
Human, but not soft.
AI-native, but still executive-safe.

RULES
• Use navy for authority, structure, and executive identity.
• Use gold sparingly: section labels, thin accents, premium emphasis, never large body blocks.
• Use teal for AI/data/network/product visuals.
• Use green only for outcomes, value creation, savings, completion, or success.
• Use plum only for selected case-study variation or strategic emphasis.
3. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
Use a simple, durable type system that renders cleanly in Word, PowerPoint, Canva, and PDF. Prefer
Aptos/Inter/Avenir-style sans serif fonts. Avoid decorative fonts and dense all-caps paragraphs.
Token Use Size Range Treatment
Display XL Cover title / hero statement 34-44 pt Bold, navy
Display L Page title 24-30 pt Bold, navy
H1 Major sections 15-18 pt All caps or title case, navy
H2 Subsections/cards 11-13 pt Bold, graphite/navy
Metric KPI numbers 20-44 pt Bold, navy or white
Body Paragraph text 9-10.5 pt Regular, graphite
Caption Metadata and notes 7-8.5 pt Slate/fog gray
Label Tags, categories 7-8 pt All caps, gold/teal
TYPOGRAPHY MISTAKES TO AVOID
Do not overuse all caps, do not cram body text below 8 pt, and do not mix more than two font families. Your
documents lose premium feel when every section is shouting.
4. GRID, SPACING, AND PAGE ARCHITECTURE
Build every asset on a repeatable layout system. The documents should feel intentionally engineered, not manually
arranged.
GRID
Use a 12-column grid for slides/landscape pages and a 2-column or 3-column grid for resume/portfolio pages.
SPACING
Use 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 px spacing increments. When in doubt, add whitespace rather than another divider line.
CARDS
Use cards to group ideas. Cards should have soft borders, 8-12 px radius, and consistent internal padding.
SECTIONS
Separate major sections with whitespace, not thick lines. Use gold micro-rules only as premium accents.

8. RESUME VISUAL UPGRADE SPECIFICATION
The executive resume should become a two-page operator profile, not a dense job-history sheet. Page 1 should sell the
thesis. Page 2 should prove it.
PAGE 1 - EXECUTIVE PROOF
• Hero: name + positioning + one-line thesis.
• KPI dashboard: 6 proof points max.
• Capability map: executive strategy, revenue operations, systems architecture, financial modeling, AI enablement, PE
value creation.
• Career timeline: company logos or clean text marks, minimal dates.
• Selected outcomes: 3 value creation cards.
PAGE 2 - EVIDENCE
• Experience condensed into outcome-led role cards.
• Technology ribbon grouped by CRM/CPQ/ERP/Data/AI.
• Industry matrix and portfolio company callout.
• Education and certifications in footer, not oversized.
9. CASE STUDY PORTFOLIO UPGRADE SPECIFICATION
The case study portfolio is already the strongest visual asset. It should be refined into a premium consulting case-
book.
RECOMMENDED CASE CARD ANATOMY

• Hero metric row: 2-4 pills such as $50M+, 2M+ SKUs, 12 integrations.

10. CAREER MASTER DATABASE UPGRADE SPECIFICATION
The Career Master Database should remain a source-of-truth artifact, but visually it should look like an intelligence
repository rather than exported tables. Keep the data; change the interface.
• Add a dashboard cover page summarizing skills, roles, tools, projects, industries, and metrics.
• Use section openers before dense tables.
• Use alternating soft row fills and smaller, more controlled table typography.
• Create a glossary of standardized capability names to prevent resume language drift.
• Add a filter legend: Expert, Advanced, Proficient, Foundational with definitions.
11. DO / DO NOT
DO DO NOT
Use large metrics and short labels. Do not fill every inch of the page.
Use whitespace as a design element. Do not use generic stock handshake imagery.
Use cards instead of long divider lines. Do not mix icon styles.
Group evidence by business outcome. Do not let bullets dominate every page.
Let one accent color lead per section. Do not use more than 6 KPI cards at once.
Make every visual prove a point. Do not make AI visuals look sci-fi or gimmicky.
