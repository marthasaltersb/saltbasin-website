## Salt Basin — clarification: evidence, testimony, and stretch roles

This replaces the unresolved evidence requirement for matches below 75% in draft 1.

### User confirmation and sources

For matches below 75%, require explicit user input or approval before finalizing the recommendation. Recommend supporting documentation, but accept the user’s own testimony as a primary source within Salt Basin.

For degrees and certifications, recommend original supporting documents, such as a diploma or a certificate issued by the relevant vendor or institution.

Maintain distinct provenance for **user-attested**, **document-supported**, and **independently verified** information. Accepting testimony must not imply that Salt Basin independently verified the claim.

Record the user’s responsibility for the accuracy of their statements and approvals. This product requirement does not itself establish a legal exemption from responsibility for Salt Basin.

### Respectful transparency

When testimony is the supporting source, use neutral language such as **“User-confirmed”** or **“Approved by the applicant.”** Do not disparage the user or label their testimony as inadequate merely because no document was uploaded.

Retain the underlying source and approval history. The placement of these disclosures in application outputs remains a design decision.

### Stretch-role application path

A user may explicitly say they lack a qualification or experience but still wish to apply. Allow them to proceed after acknowledging that choice.

In this path:

- Preserve truthful resume language grounded in the user’s actual experience and attested skills.
- Do not invent transferable skills, qualifications, years of experience, or proficiency levels.
- Do not treat “I feel ready” as testimony that the user possesses a missing credential or skill.
- Explain that a lower match may reduce ATS alignment without claiming a known probability of rejection.
- Express career-transition intent honestly where appropriate, without turning aspirations into experience claims.

Approval to apply is separate from confirmation that a particular qualification is true. A high match score also never authorizes fabrication.

### Longitudinal history

Preserve a traceable history of sources, user statements, wording approvals, revisions, and prior use across applications.

This history can demonstrate consistency and provenance over time. Repeated use or the passage of years must not automatically convert a user-attested claim into an independently verified one.

### Interface test cases

Verify that a user can:

- Confirm a truthful claim through testimony without uploading documentation.
- Apply for a stretch role while explicitly acknowledging a missing qualification.
- Proceed without the system adding unsupported claims to improve ATS matching.
- Inspect the source and approval history behind recommended language.
- Distinguish user confirmation from documentary support and independent verification.
