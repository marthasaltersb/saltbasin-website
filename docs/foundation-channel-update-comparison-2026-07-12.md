## Guardrails retained from prior prompts

- Never mechanically replace conversational uses of “journey.”
- A Git branch, configuration-tree branch, geometric branch, or typed relationship is not a Channel Tributary.
- Authoritative evidence is preserved. Compositions, settlement, Channel position, projections, divergence, hashes, and Agent interpretations are derived and reconstructable.
- Revenue, Customer, and Member Channels are correlated but intentionally non-equivalent.
- Proximity, persistence, cache state, or background-agent access never increases the interacting user’s authority.
- BestyStaff is intentional spelling and is never renamed.
- The Salter Momentum™ spine remains Understanding → Rendering → Manifesting. The retired five-pillar invention remains retired.

## Items kept product-specific rather than promoted to universal Foundation law

- Exact Channel stage names are configurable seed definitions, not universal ontology.
- Exact camera speeds, delays, geometries, formulas, and simulator inputs belong in versioned registries.
- The Account Executive and private-equity examples are canonical scenarios, not the only permitted Channel types.
- Server-rack and cloud topology are Infrastructure World variants, not competing core semantic models.
