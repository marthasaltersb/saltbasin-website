# Salt Basin Brand Context

Purpose: define the reusable visual, verbal, and product-interface standards for Salt Basin Net Works, SaltTide, HandoverOS, MESA, BestyStaff, and related operating-intelligence outputs.

Use this context whenever creating or reviewing Salt Basin websites, decks, PDFs, Word documents, product mockups, admin interfaces, public agents, executive summaries, architecture diagrams, or reusable templates.

## Brand Thesis

Salt Basin is an operating-intelligence brand: calm, precise, rooted, executive, and visually distinctive. The work should feel like a living source of truth for business handoffs, revenue decisions, portfolio evidence, and reusable executive artifacts.

Core line:

```text
We build for the customer you keep, not just the deal you close.
```

Supporting idea:

```text
Follow the network, not the object model.
```

Faith-rooted anchor:

```text
Matthew 5:13. Build work that carries flavor and does not lose it.
```

## Visual Direction

Use an editorial operating-intelligence aesthetic:

- Warm cream and parchment grounds.
- Ink or navy structural sections.
- Teal for intelligence, systems, and calm control.
- Terracotta gold for emphasis, trust, proof, and financial signal.
- Dusty pink only as a human, creative, or accent signal. Do not use it as a dominant fill.
- Fine lines, restrained shadows, dense but readable grids, and no generic SaaS landing-page gloss.

Avoid:

- Purple/blue gradient dominance.
- Generic marketing hero cards.
- Decorative orbs, bokeh, blobs, and vague atmosphere.
- Copy that promises more than the evidence supports.
- One-off visuals that cannot become reusable templates.

## Typography

Preferred web pairing:

- Display: Cormorant Garamond for Salt Basin public brand moments.
- Alternative display: Fraunces for executive templates, architecture templates, and artifact systems.
- Body and labels: Jost or DM Sans.
- Technical labels: JetBrains Mono only when showing metrics, data, IDs, or evidence.

Use display type for true heroes and major editorial section titles. Use smaller, tighter labels in dashboards, admin panels, and tools.

## Motifs

The reusable Salt Basin visual motif is a crystal system:

- Signature crystal: primary Salt Basin mark and closing brand anchor.
- Hourglass crystal: SaltTide, Credit Health Infrastructure, route timing, and customer financial decision intelligence.
- Engine or knot crystal: HandoverOS, the Salt Basin Highway Operating System for journey data rods, SaltBridge lineage, SaltTide routing, and operating network handoffs.
- Interlocking rings: MESA, advisory, partnership, and network concepts.

Use these as visual anchors, not decoration. If 3D rendering would add fragile dependencies, CSS, canvas, or static exported images are acceptable.

## Product Architecture Language

Use these names consistently:

- Salt Basin Net Works: the platform, practice, public site, and operator network.
- SaltTide: Credit Health Infrastructure.
- HandoverOS: Salt Basin Highway Operating System. It covers journey data rods in SaltBridge, SaltTide paths, and customer handoff networks.
- MESA: management, execution, strategy, and assessment advisory services.
- BestyStaff: Betsy's transparent AI proxy and routing agent.
- The Salter Momentum: the transformation method: Understanding, Rendering, Manifesting.

## Public Copy Rules

Write in an executive, precise, human voice. Prefer concrete nouns and evidence over hype.

Allowed public claims:

- Salt Basin works on quote-to-revenue handoffs, data integrity, operating model design, executive artifacts, and safe previews.
- Betsy has experience across enterprise systems, revenue operations, CPQ, billing, financial modeling, and transformation.
- The site acts as a living portfolio and reusable output surface.

Guardrails:

- Do not claim ownership of work products created under past employers or client projects.
- Do not expose proprietary client details, workbook internals, full taxonomies, private formulas, employer-owned material, or source artifacts.
- Investor, credit, and financial advisory language must be supportable and reviewed before being made public.

## Interface Patterns

Use these patterns across Salt Basin artifacts:

- KPI tiles for proof.
- Metadata chips for industry, role, system, confidence, status, and source.
- L3/L4 process maps for operational flow.
- Capability cards for reusable product functions.
- Scenario to capability tables for architecture mapping.
- Executive summary panels with configurable financial exposure, recommendation, risk, and timeline blocks.
- Network cards for operator profiles.

Keep interactive tools feature-complete enough to be useful. Do not put explanatory text on screen that merely describes how the UI works.
