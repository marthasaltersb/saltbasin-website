# III. SEMANTIC INVARIANTS

Every world variant must preserve the following semantic definitions.

## QUERY RELEVANCE

How directly an element relates to the active query context.

## QUERY COVERAGE

How much applicable required semantic context is represented.

## QUERY CONFIDENCE

How strongly the system trusts the current semantic view.

## CONVERGENCE STABILITY

How likely the current convergence result is to materially change.

## JOURNEY POSITION

Where the journey currently exists in its stage and cycle.

## STAGE COMPLETENESS

How much required stage definition, metadata, evidence, and validation is present.

## STAGE READINESS

How prepared the journey is to transition to the next stage.

## ROD MATURITY

The quality and depth of definition, evidence, lineage, validation, temporal continuity, and relationships accumulated across the journey.

## JOURNEY DENSITY

The amount of differentiated meaningful history and complexity accumulated in the journey.

## ROD COHERENCE

How consistently the rod's state, definitions, evidence, and relationships agree.

## CROSS-ROD ALIGNMENT

Whether states across Revenue Lifecycle, Customer Journey, and Member Journey are logically and temporally compatible.

No world variant may redefine these metrics.

A variant may only choose a different visual representation.

# VARIANT 1 — CRYSTAL BASIN

Variant Key:

CRYSTAL_BASIN

Concept:

A living field of crystalline intelligence structures suspended within a deep dimensional basin.

This should most directly reflect the current Salt Basin crystal atom model.

## WORLD STRUCTURE

- large central basin;
- layered depth;
- crystal atoms;
- crystal molecule clusters;
- Orbits as larger gravitational crystal intelligence bodies;
- Data Rods as elongated crystalline temporal structures;
- lineage paths as illuminated crystal filaments;
- branches as growing crystal forks;
- merge points as fused crystal junctions.

## QUERY CONVERGENCE

When an Orbit is selected:

elements reorganize radially around the Orbit.

Distance represents Query Relevance.

Semantic bands:

CORE
STRONGLY RELATED
CONTEXTUAL
PERIPHERAL

The points should converge into organized dimensional shells rather than one dense ball.

## CONFIDENCE

Confidence may control crystal clarity.

High confidence:

clear crystalline surface.

Low confidence:

diffuse, refracted, visually uncertain internal structure.

Do not use simple opacity if it makes the object unreadable.

## STABILITY

Low stability:

subtle bounded internal oscillation.

High stability:

crystal settles into a visually anchored structure.

## MATURITY

Maturity controls internal geometric complexity.

Low maturity:

simple crystal geometry.

High maturity:

more layered facets, internal lattice, nested geometry, or structured crystalline growth.

Do not simply scale the crystal larger.

## LINEAGE

Lineage appears as connected luminous crystalline filaments.

Observed or validated relationship:

continuous path.

Inferred relationship:

segmented or refracted path.

## RODS

Data Rods appear as long crystallized dimensional spines.

Stages are visible as structural segments.

Stage completeness may appear as filled internal lattice within the active segment.

Rod maturity should influence the complexity of the entire rod's internal crystal structure.

# VARIANT 2 — ORBITAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM

Variant Key:

ORBITAL_INTELLIGENCE

Concept:

A gravitational semantic solar system where selected Orbits become active contextual bodies and related intelligence assumes orbit based on relevance.

Do not copy a literal sci-fi solar system.

The visual language should remain modern, abstract, executive-grade, and Salt Basin-specific.

## WORLD STRUCTURE

- Orbits as contextual gravity bodies;
- atoms as intelligence bodies;
- molecules as orbital constellations;
- rods as temporal arcs or elongated orbital tracks;
- lineage as trajectories;
- branches as diverging orbital paths;
- branch merges as trajectory intersections.

## QUERY CONVERGENCE

Selecting Customer activates Customer as the current semantic gravity center.

Query Relevance controls orbital shell radius.

Highest relevance:

inner orbit.

Lowest relevance:

outer orbit.

Use stable orbital shells or curved spatial regions.

Do not make every node continuously spin at high speed.

The environment must remain readable.

## COVERAGE

Coverage may influence the completeness of the visible contextual orbit field.

For example:

missing required context dimensions appear as clearly marked empty orbital sectors or unresolved shells.

Do not imply that an empty sector is missing data unless the semantic coverage model supports it.

## CONFIDENCE

Confidence may influence orbital path precision.

High-confidence relationships:

stable precise orbital paths.

Low-confidence relationships:

broader orbital uncertainty bands.

## STABILITY

Low convergence stability:

objects may transition between nearby allowable orbit ranges.

High stability:

orbit locks.

## MATURITY

Maturity may control the complexity and persistence of a body's orbital history trails.

More mature rods and journey states retain structured temporal trajectory histories.

## RODS

Revenue, Customer, and Member Rods may render as three parallel dimensional orbital arcs around an outcome or organization context.

They should remain visually interdependent but independently staged.

Cross-Rod Alignment is visualized through aligned relationship bridges between compatible state positions.

Misalignment appears as tension or angular divergence, not necessarily as a red error.
