# Rejected Patterns

- Metaphor-first design and decorative semantics.
- Per-scene business logic or scattered variant branches.
- A second path-color system or redefined metric meaning.
- Collapsing domain worlds and spatial variants.
- Dense layouts that destroy shells, lineage, or topology.
- Unnamed red walls, dead spatial links, pointer-only navigation, and irreversible camera traps.
- Floating 3D spreadsheets or forms.
- Unrelated mascots for each agent.
- Cinematic movement without semantic or navigational value.
- Promoting experiments to canonical behavior without approval and evidence.
